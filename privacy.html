Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Short version: SHARP does not collect any data. Every photo is taken and processed on your iPhone and stays there unless you decide to share it.

What SHARP does on your device

  • Camera is used only while the camera screen is open, to show the preview and take photos. All processing runs on your iPhone.
  • Motion sensors are read while the camera is open to detect camera shake and pick the best moment and frames. These readings are not stored except as technical details of a capture.
  • Face detection in NO BLUR mode finds faces and eyes only to decide which frame is sharpest. It never identifies anyone, and nothing about faces leaves your iPhone.
  • Photos: SHARP saves results to your photo library if you turn that on. It only asks for permission to add photos and cannot see your existing library.
  • Your captures (results, comparison images, technical diagnostics and, if you turn it on, the original frames) are stored inside the app on your device. You can delete them in Settings; deleting the app deletes them too.

What SHARP does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • No uploads: SHARP has no server and makes no network connections. Your photos never leave your device through SHARP.

Sharing

When you share a photo through the system share sheet, the app you choose receives it, and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Children

SHARP collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

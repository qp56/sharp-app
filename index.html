Frequently asked

Which mode should I use?

SUPER DETAIL for landscapes, buildings and text. NO BLUR for people and anything that moves. ALL FOCUS for close-ups where you want near and far in focus. AUTO is a normal single photo.

Why does SUPER DETAIL take a few seconds?

It aligns and merges several full-resolution frames on your iPhone. Hold the phone as you normally would; the small natural movement of your hand is what adds extra detail.

Why was my result labelled "Upscaled" or used fewer frames?

SHARP measures what it achieved. Frames that are shaky or don't line up are left out, and a higher resolution is only called super-resolution when the frames really contain the extra detail. Open the result and tap Diagnostics to see the details.

Where are my photos?

In the gallery inside SHARP. With Save results to Photos on (Settings), every result is also saved to your photo library.

Does SHARP upload my photos?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
